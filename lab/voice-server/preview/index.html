What changes

  • One catalogue and lifecycle. Pocket is the service. Alfred is one available voice. The catalog comes from the available Pocket profiles.
  • No duplicate realtime models. REST and realtime reuse the same transcription and synthesis workers.
  • One synthesis engine. Pocket provides speech synthesis with selectable voice profiles. Parakeet provides transcription.
  • One service record and backup. Alfred is a voice of Pocket, with one service lifecycle and backup.
